# Demo Script

## Five-Minute Walkthrough

1. Open the dashboard and show total calls, booked appointments, RAG answers, and `$0` local platform cost.
2. Open Playground.
3. Send: `Hi, I am Lina. I need teeth cleaning next Tuesday morning. My phone is 555-0100.`
4. Show the assistant booking the appointment and speaking the response when browser TTS is supported.
5. Open Calls and show transcript, summary, and outcome.
6. Open Appointments and show the booked appointment.
7. Open Knowledge and show BrightCare Dental FAQ.
8. Open Campaigns and explain local outbound simulation.
9. Open Settings and explain Groq LLM mode, browser voice, and production telephony upgrade path.

## Proposal Positioning

Describe it as a modular open-source AI voice platform demo for inbound reception, outbound campaign simulation, appointment booking, RAG answers, and analytics.
